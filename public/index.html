Frequently asked questions

        

          

            What kind of Instagram content can I download?
            
Public posts, reels, IGTV videos, and stories. The account has to be public — private content can't be fetched.

          

          

            Do I need to log in to Instagram?
            
No. Just paste the link to the public post or reel you want to save.

          

          

            Is it free?
            
Yes, completely free to use.

          

          

            Can I download just the audio?
            
Yes — use the "Download Audio (MP3)" button to save just the sound from a video.

          

          

            Why did my download fail?
            
Make sure the account is public and the link points directly to a post, reel, or story. Private or deleted content can't be downloaded.
